## Post 1: announcement

Anonymous group votes on Cardano, verified on-chain. Voters prove membership with a
zero-knowledge proof; nobody can tell who voted for what. No wallet needed for voters. Open
source, audited, live on Preprod.

Try it: https://vote.trustlevel.io
Guides: https://github.com/TrustLevel/ZK-Voting-App

## Post 2: what testing changed

Our testers' biggest hurdle was the wallet. So we removed it: the browser builds the ZK proof,
a relay wallet pays the vote. Voters need an e-mail link and nothing else. Interim results are
hidden until the end, at their request too.
